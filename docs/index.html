Levoit TempSense 36 Pro Remote IR APK for Android

Android IR remote app for Xiaomi and Redmi phones with IR blaster. Replacement remote control for Levoit TempSense 36 Pro tower fan.

This unofficial app sends NEC infrared commands through Android's native ConsumerIrManager API. It is made for Android phones with built-in IR hardware and may also work with compatible external IR transmitters.

Features

  • Power, swing, timer, sleep mode, sound, mode, and fan speed controls.
  • NEC MSB infrared codes tested on a Redmi phone with integrated IR.
  • Local sync controls to match the app display with the real tower state.
  • Saved screen flip mode for external IR adapter setups.
  • Offline control without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, account login, or pairing.

Device Support

Requires an Android phone with built-in IR hardware, such as compatible Xiaomi or Redmi models, or an external IR transmitter that exposes itself through Android's consumer IR API.
